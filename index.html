How the planner works

1. Scenarios. For each delivery hour, the RT−DA spreads at the same hour over a trailing 30-day window, lagged two days so the plan never sees prices it could not have known at DAM time.

2. Hourly CVaR LP. A linear program splits the block into planned-off, economic flex, Reg-Up / Reg-Down / RRS / ECRS / Non-Spin capacity and a day-ahead hedge, minimising expected cost + weight × CVaR (Rockafellar–Uryasev), solved by a dependency-free simplex.

3. SLA as a price. The monthly curtailment budget becomes a Lagrange multiplier from an empirical price quantile, plus a within-day reservation and a real-time guard that counts ramp-recovery hours.

4. Honest replay. Settlement at actual LZ_WEST prices with ramp limits and deployments. The hindsight bound sees realised prices and ignores ramp limits: an upper bound, not a target.

Data: ERCOT MIS public reports 13060, 13061, 13091.
