How the planner works

Everything on this screen is computed in your browser from the public ERCOT files; nothing is hard-coded.
1 · ScenariosFor each delivery hour, the RT−DA spreads at the same hour over a trailing 30-day window, lagged two days so the plan never sees prices it could not have known at DAM time.
2 · Hourly CVaR LPA linear program splits the block into planned-off, economic flex (curtail if RT > threshold), REGUP / REGDN / RRS / ECRS / Non-Spin capacity and a day-ahead energy hedge, minimising expected cost + weight × CVaR (Rockafellar–Uryasev). Solved by a dependency-free simplex.
3 · SLA as a priceThe monthly curtailment budget becomes a Lagrange multiplier set from an empirical price quantile of the remaining budget, plus a chronological within-day reservation and a hard real-time guard that includes ramp-recovery hours.
4 · Honest replayReal-time settlement at actual LZ_WEST prices with ramp limits, deployments, and budget enforcement. The hindsight bound sees realised prices and ignores ramp limits, so it is an upper bound, not a target.
